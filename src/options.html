Privacy & Local Processing

100% Local Inference: Every image analyzed by ZoodSle is processed locally on your Apple Silicon Mac using qwen/qwen3-vl-4b in LM Studio. No photos, descriptions, or conversation logs are sent to cloud services, external AI providers, or remote analytics.

Safari Permissions: Access to http://127.0.0.1:8000/* is used strictly for the local vision backend, and standard web host permissions allow inspecting photos and presenting descriptions across any webpage.
